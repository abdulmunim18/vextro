# VEXTRO UI Design Direction

## Visual Style

VEXTRO shall use a clean, modern AI-commerce SaaS interface.

### Primary characteristics

- White and light-gray surfaces
- Purple primary brand color
- Dark navy headings and navigation
- Green for positive results and successful recommendations
- Orange for warnings
- Red for risks, negative gaps and failures
- Rounded cards and controls
- Subtle borders and shadows
- Clear data tables and charts
- Desktop-first responsive layout

## Public Website

The public landing page shall clearly communicate that VEXTRO serves:

1. Consumers
2. SMEs and business users

The landing page shall contain:

- Hero section
- Product value proposition
- Consumer benefits
- SME benefits
- Feature cards
- AI intelligence preview
- Login and registration actions

## Consumer Application

The consumer interface shall include:

- Consumer dashboard
- Product search and filtering
- Product details
- Cross-platform comparison
- Price history
- Price alerts
- Watchlist
- Recommendations
- Review and AI insights

## SME Application

The SME interface shall include:

- SME overview dashboard
- Inventory monitoring
- Competitor tracking
- Competitor pricing gaps
- Demand forecasting
- Reorder recommendations
- Pricing advisor
- Reports and alerts

## Administrator Application

The administrator interface shall include:

- System dashboard
- User management
- Product and listing management
- Product-match review
- Scraper-run monitoring
- Error logs
- Model information
- Reports

## Navigation

Consumer, SME and Administrator users shall have separate sidebar navigation based on their role.

## Design Status

The current visual references are approved as the initial UI direction.

They are conceptual references and may be adjusted during implementation where required for usability, responsiveness or technical feasibility.
